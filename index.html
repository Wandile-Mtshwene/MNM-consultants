About MNM Consultants

We Level the
Playing Field

Large corporates have access to armies of lawyers, employment specialists, risk officers, compliance experts, and financial advisors. Small and medium businesses often go without — not because these services aren't needed, but because there's never been a single, accessible point of entry.

MNM Consultants was built to change that. We work exclusively with South African SMEs, giving them the same quality of advice and implementation support that enterprise businesses take for granted — without the enterprise price tag.

From registering your first entity at CIPC, to navigating your BEE scorecard, to setting up your employment framework and ensuring you're audit-ready — we're with you every step of the way.

"One Partner. Every Solution. Endless Growth." — That's not a tagline. That's a commitment we make to every business we work with.

  • Commercial contracts & MOUs
  • Intellectual property protection
  • CCMA representation & support

Employment & Labour

Connecting businesses with the right people, and protecting both employer and employee through every stage of the employment relationship.

  • Recruitment & candidate selection
  • Employment contracts & offer letters
  • Employee rights advisory (LRA & BCEA)
  • Employee benefits & UIF structuring
  • Employment Equity (EEA) compliance
  • Disciplinary, grievance & CCMA support

Risk Management

Identifying threats before they become crises — and building the systems that keep your business resilient when they do.

  • Business risk assessments
  • Risk registers & governance frameworks
  • Business continuity planning
  • Insurance advisory & structuring
  • COIDA & workplace safety compliance
  • Operational risk controls
04

Compliance

Staying ahead of an ever-changing regulatory landscape, so your business never gets caught off-guard or underprepared.

  • PAYE, UIF & COIDA submissions
  • OHSA health & safety compliance
